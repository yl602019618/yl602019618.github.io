# About Me

<img src="https://yl602019618.github.io/zhijunzeng.jpg" class="floatpic">

Here is **Zhijun Zeng (Bob, 曾祉竣)**.<br>

Hello, I’m Zhijun Zeng (Bob). I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computational Mathematics at the Yau Mathematical Sciences Center, Tsinghua University, advised by [Prof. Zuoqiang Shi](https://shizqi.github.io/) and Prof. Yi Zhu. I was also a visiting scholar with [Prof. Yunan Yang](https://yunany.github.io/) at Cornell University in Fall 2024. Before joining Tsinghua, I earned my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from the Shanghai University of Finance and Economics.

My research focuses on computational inverse problems, ultrasound imaging, nonlinear filtering, knowledge discovery and system identification, diffusion language models, and science agents. I am always happy to discuss ideas and collaborations. Please feel free to reach out at **zengzj22 [at] mails.tsinghua.edu.cn**.
